| Product | Grain | Purpose | Authoritative Silver sources | Consumption intent |

The representative SLA rule selects breached, then escalated, then latest-due,
then lowest stable SLA identifier. A missing SLA remains `NO_SLA_EVIDENCE`.

## Governed metric definitions

`GOVERNANCE.GOLD_METRIC_CATALOG` versions the ten required definitions:
`PAYMENT_COUNT`, `EXCEPTION_COUNT`, `HIGH_VALUE_PAYMENT_COUNT`,
`HIGH_VALUE_EXCEPTION_COUNT`, `EXCEPTION_RATE`,
`HIGH_VALUE_EXCEPTION_RATE`, `UNRESOLVED_EXCEPTION_COUNT`,
`SLA_BREACH_COUNT`, `SLA_BREACH_RATE`, and
`ESCALATION_REQUIRED_COUNT`.

Counts are additive only across compatible non-overlapping dimensions. Rates
are recomputed from numerator and denominator with `NULLIF`; precomputed rates
must never be averaged. Amounts retain `CURRENCY_CODE`; no FX normalization is
approved, so cross-currency monetary totals are prohibited.

`HIGH_VALUE_EXCEPTION_RATE = HIGH_VALUE_EXCEPTION_COUNT /
NULLIF(HIGH_VALUE_PAYMENT_COUNT, 0)`. It answers: “Of the high-value payments,
what percentage became exceptions?” The alternative ratio
`HIGH_VALUE_EXCEPTION_COUNT / EXCEPTION_COUNT` answers a different question and
is not the approved lighthouse metric. A zero high-value-payment denominator
produces `NULL`, not a fabricated zero.

## Period semantics

- `INVESTIGATION_WEEK`: 2026-03-09 through 2026-03-15.
- `PREVIOUS_WEEK`: 2026-03-02 through 2026-03-08.
- `TRAILING_FOUR_WEEK`: 2026-02-09 through 2026-03-08.
- `HISTORICAL_BASELINE`: records through 2026-02-08.

Comparison-window rows intentionally overlap: previous week is part of the
trailing-four-week window. These rows are independently calculated and cannot
be summed. The case-level primary classification is mutually exclusive: its
`TRAILING_FOUR_WEEK` label covers 2026-02-09 through 2026-03-01 after the
separate previous-week assignment. The deterministic spike test recomputes
weighted rates from counts and does not hard-code expected percentages.

## Investigation priority

Priority is deterministic business logic, not an ML or risk model:

- `CRITICAL`: unresolved, high-value, and breached or escalation-required.
- `HIGH`: unresolved and high-value, breached, escalation-required, or approved
  severity `CRITICAL`/`HIGH`.
- `MEDIUM`: otherwise unresolved and manual-review-required or at least three
  days old at the approved 2026-03-15 reference date.
- `STANDARD`: all other trusted cases.

`PRIORITY_REASON` exposes the contributing attributes for explanation.

## Sensitive-data minimization

Gold excludes customer name, email, phone, postal address, raw customer/account
identifiers, beneficiary details, and direct assigned-user identifiers. Case
investigation retains stable SHA-256 customer/account/user references only where
needed for correlation. Payment amount remains solely for approved investigation
and currency-grained metrics. Workload products expose team context, not user
payload. Audit tables store identifiers and counts, not business payload.

## Governance and eligibility

All five products receive `DATA_LAYER=GOLD`, `SYNTHETIC_DATA=TRUE`, approved
domain, `OWNER_DOMAIN=BANKING_OPERATIONS`,
`PERMITTED_PURPOSE=TRUSTED_BANKING_OPERATIONS_AI`, classification, and
sensitivity tags using the existing taxonomy. Product metadata records business
purpose, owner, steward, definition version, freshness, fitness, limitations,
consumption intent, and potential AI eligibility.

Gold eligibility is not AI-context authorization. Future TB-06 must still
evaluate user, persona, purpose, necessity, minimization, eligibility, and
system/prompt constraints. `Retrieved ≠ Admitted to AI Context` remains binding.

## Access design

- `TRUSTED_AI_DATA_ENGINEER`: transformation DML and Gold evidence writes.
- `TRUSTED_AI_DATA_STEWARD`: read-only products and governed definitions.
- `TRUSTED_AI_AUDITOR`: minimized run/product evidence and definitions, not Gold
  business payload.
- `TRUSTED_AI_BRONZE_READER`: no Gold access.
- `TRUSTED_AI_TAG_ADMIN`: tag administration without business-data SELECT.

A dedicated operations-consumer role is deferred until its persona-to-role
contract is approved. `ACCOUNTADMIN` is never application-consumption authority.

## Audit, lineage, validation, and idempotency

Each run has a Gold transformation ID, trace ID, role, timestamps, status, query
ID, and sanitized error field. Each product records Silver run/trace references,
source/output counts, validation failures, status, and timestamp. Case rows link
to Silver transformation and trace identifiers; Silver retains Bronze ingestion,
record, and source-file lineage. This supports Gold → Silver → Bronze → source
reconstruction without copying sensitive payload into audit.

Validation checks five objects, grains, keys, independent Silver reconciliation,
metric math, currency safety, periods, priority, deterministic spike, anchors,
minimization, lineage, governance, access, and evidence. Runtime PASS is deferred
to G2.5E.

For unchanged Silver input, rerun `033` in a new session. Gold uses
`INSERT OVERWRITE`, so keys, counts, metrics, and products must remain identical;
the new run and five evidence rows append to audit history. Compare before/after
counts and metric numerators/denominators, and prove no duplicate product keys.

## Future readiness and limitations

Stable grains, centralized definitions, deterministic relationships, explicit
dates/currencies, and governed descriptions prepare future Semantic Views and
controlled analytical capabilities such as exception context, trends, SLA
exposure, and workload. G2.5 creates no Semantic View, Cortex Analyst object,
AI context gate, function, procedure, API, Agent tool, Agent, or AI invocation.

Known limitations: no FX conversion; comparison periods are synthetic-reference
windows; product freshness is batch-bound to approved Silver execution; a
consumer-role design remains pending; potential AI eligibility requires future
TB-06 authorization.

## Read-only business demo queries

### 1. Investigation week versus previous week

```sql
SELECT PERIOD_NAME,SUM(EXCEPTION_COUNT) EXCEPTIONS,SUM(PAYMENT_COUNT) PAYMENTS,
       SUM(EXCEPTION_COUNT)/NULLIF(SUM(PAYMENT_COUNT),0) EXCEPTION_RATE
FROM TRUSTED_AI_GOVERNANCE.GOLD.PAYMENT_EXCEPTION_PERIOD_COMPARISON
WHERE PERIOD_NAME IN ('INVESTIGATION_WEEK','PREVIOUS_WEEK')
GROUP BY PERIOD_NAME;
```

### 2. High-value exception spike

```sql
SELECT PERIOD_NAME,SUM(HIGH_VALUE_EXCEPTION_COUNT) HIGH_VALUE_EXCEPTIONS,
       SUM(HIGH_VALUE_PAYMENT_COUNT) HIGH_VALUE_PAYMENTS,
       SUM(HIGH_VALUE_EXCEPTION_COUNT)/NULLIF(SUM(HIGH_VALUE_PAYMENT_COUNT),0) HIGH_VALUE_EXCEPTION_RATE
FROM TRUSTED_AI_GOVERNANCE.GOLD.PAYMENT_EXCEPTION_PERIOD_COMPARISON
GROUP BY PERIOD_NAME;
```

### 3. Reasons driving the investigation-week increase

```sql
SELECT EXCEPTION_REASON_CODE,COUNT(*) EXCEPTION_COUNT
FROM TRUSTED_AI_GOVERNANCE.GOLD.PAYMENT_EXCEPTION_INVESTIGATION
WHERE PRIMARY_PERIOD_CLASSIFICATION='INVESTIGATION_WEEK'
GROUP BY EXCEPTION_REASON_CODE ORDER BY EXCEPTION_COUNT DESC;
```

### 4. Breached and high-priority SLA cases

```sql
SELECT EXCEPTION_ID,SLA_EVENT_ID,INVESTIGATION_PRIORITY,SLA_BREACH_MINUTES,
       OWNING_TEAM_NAME
FROM TRUSTED_AI_GOVERNANCE.GOLD.SLA_OPERATIONAL_EXPOSURE
WHERE SLA_BREACHED_FLAG OR INVESTIGATION_PRIORITY IN ('CRITICAL','HIGH')
ORDER BY SLA_BREACH_MINUTES DESC;
```

### 5. Team workload

```sql
SELECT OWNING_TEAM_ID,OWNING_TEAM_NAME,SUM(OPEN_EXCEPTION_COUNT) OPEN_CASES,
       SUM(HIGH_VALUE_OPEN_COUNT) HIGH_VALUE_OPEN,
       SUM(SLA_BREACH_OPEN_COUNT) BREACHED_OPEN
FROM TRUSTED_AI_GOVERNANCE.GOLD.OPERATIONS_WORKLOAD_SUMMARY
GROUP BY OWNING_TEAM_ID,OWNING_TEAM_NAME ORDER BY OPEN_CASES DESC;
```

### 6. Governed context for `EXC_DEMO_001`

```sql
SELECT EXCEPTION_ID,PAYMENT_ID,EXCEPTION_REASON_CODE,INVESTIGATION_PRIORITY,
       PRIORITY_REASON,HIGH_VALUE_FLAG,SLA_BREACHED_FLAG,SLA_BREACH_MINUTES,
       OWNING_TEAM_NAME,TRACE_ID,SOURCE_SILVER_TRANSFORMATION_RUN_ID
FROM TRUSTED_AI_GOVERNANCE.GOLD.PAYMENT_EXCEPTION_INVESTIGATION
WHERE EXCEPTION_ID='EXC_DEMO_001';
```
